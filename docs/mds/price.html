mds:price

Money in Toman or Rial, with Persian digits and separators.

Persian digits, the ٬ thousands separator and the currency label, from one amount.

۲٬۵۰۰٬۰۰۰ تومان 
<mds:price :amount="2500000" />

With an original price

Pass original and you get the strikethrough and the discount badge for free — the percentage is computed, not passed in.

۲۲٪
۳٬۲۰۰٬۰۰۰
۲٬۵۰۰٬۰۰۰ تومان 
<mds:price :amount="2500000" :original="3200000" size="lg" />

Latin digits

:fa="false" switches one price to Latin digits and grouping. To do it everywhere, set config('mds.persian_digits') to false — every mds component reads it at render time, which is how the English demo is built.

1,500,000

1,200,000 Toman 
<div class="flex flex-wrap items-center gap-8">
    <mds:price :amount="1200000" :original="1500000" :fa="false" />
    <mds:price :amount="1200000" :fa="false" currency="Toman" />
</div>

Blade directives

For money inside a sentence, the directives are shorter than the component.

قیمت این کالا ۲٬۵۰۰٬۰۰۰ تومان است.

<flux:text>قیمت این کالا @toman(2500000) است.</flux:text>

Reference

mds:price

PropDescription
amountThe price. Default: 0.
originalPre-discount price; adds the strikethrough and the badge.
currencytoman, rial, none, or a literal label. Default: config('mds.currency').
decimalsDecimal places. Default: 0.
sizeOptions: sm, lg.
faPersian digits. Default: config('mds.persian_digits').
badgeShows the discount badge when original is set. Default: true.

@toman / @rial

Blade directives for a formatted amount inline, e.g. @toman($order->total).
