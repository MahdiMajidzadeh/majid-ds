mds:preview-card

A hover preview of a link's destination, shown beside it.

The trigger is a real <a> — it still navigates on click. Hovering or focusing it reveals a card previewing where the link goes: the profile behind an @mention, the product behind a link. Hover the mention below.

Keep the preview supplementary. The card only appears on hover or keyboard focus — it never opens on touch, and screen readers do not announce it. It previews where the link goes; anything essential belongs on the linked page itself.

Media preview

The content is just a styled container, so a card can lead with a cover image: cancel the padding with !p-0, clip with overflow-hidden, pad the text back in.

Side and alignment

Position with side (top / bottom / start / end) and align (start / center / end). Logical sides on purpose: start/end and the alignment axis mirror on RTL pages by themselves. The card flips and shifts automatically to stay in view.

Delays

Hover waits delay before opening and close-delay before closing, so the card neither flickers on a passing cursor nor vanishes on the way to it. Keyboard focus opens immediately — focusing a link is intentional — and Escape always closes.

An impatient preview next to a patient one.

Reference

mds:preview-card

The root. Holds the open state; renders an inline wrapper.

PropDescription
delayHover-open delay in milliseconds. Default: 600.
close-delayHover-close delay in milliseconds. Default: 300.

mds:preview-card.trigger

The inline anchor that owns the link — it navigates on click like any <a>.

PropDescription
hrefNavigation target. Any other anchor attribute (target, rel, …) passes through.

mds:preview-card.content

The auto-positioned popup — teleported to <body> (so a block-level card can legally live inside a paragraph) and position: fixed.

PropDescription
sidePreferred side: top, bottom, start, end. Flips when out of room. Default: bottom.
alignAlignment along that side: start, center, end. Default: center.
side-offsetGap between the link and the card. Default: 10, or 6 without the arrow.
arrowThe pointer toward the link. Default: true.
