A live countdown to a deadline.

Reference

mds:countdown

PropDescription
untilDeadline: a Carbon instance, a timestamp or a parseable string.
daysShows a days segment. Default: true.
labelsShows the unit labels under the digits. Default: false.
sizeOptions: sm, lg.
expired-textShown once the deadline passes.
faPersian digits. Default: config('mds.persian_digits').
